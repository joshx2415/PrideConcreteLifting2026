# FAQ Questions for Pride Concrete Lifting

**Instructions for the owner:** Answer these in your own words. Short answers are fine — even a couple sentences. Don't worry about grammar or polish; we'll clean everything up. Just answer like you're talking to a homeowner who called you on the phone.

---

## General / About the Business

1. How long have you been lifting concrete in Texas?
2. What got you into the concrete lifting business?
3. What areas do you serve? (List specific cities/neighborhoods if you can)
4. Are you licensed and insured?
5. Do you offer free estimates?
6. How quickly can you typically get out to look at a job after someone calls?

## The Process / How It Works

7. What is polyurethane foam injection, in simple terms?
8. How long does a typical residential job take start to finish?
9. How soon can I walk or drive on the concrete after you're done?
10. Will you need to tear up my yard or driveway to do the work?
11. How big are the holes you drill? Can you see them when it's done?
12. Is the foam you use safe? Is it toxic or harmful to pets, kids, or landscaping?
13. How long does the foam last? Will I need to have this done again in a few years?

## Cost & Value

14. How much does concrete lifting typically cost compared to tearing out and replacing?
15. What's the typical price range for a residential driveway job? (Ballpark is fine)
16. Do you offer financing or payment plans?
17. What factors affect the cost of a job?

## Foam vs. Mudjacking vs. Replacement

18. What's the difference between what you do and mudjacking?
19. Why do you prefer foam over mudjacking?
20. When would you actually recommend tearing out and replacing concrete instead of lifting it?
21. Is there a situation where concrete is too far gone to lift?

## Driveways

22. What causes driveways to sink in the DFW area specifically?
23. How do I know if my driveway needs lifting vs. full replacement?
24. Can you fix a driveway that has large cracks, or just sunken sections?

## Sidewalks & Trip Hazards

25. My city sent me a notice about my uneven sidewalk. Can you fix that?
26. What's the biggest trip hazard you've ever fixed?
27. Can you work around tree roots without killing the tree?

## Pool Decks

28. Can foam be used near water/pools? Does it react with pool chemicals?
29. How common is it for pool decks to sink in North Texas?
30. Can you fix an infinity pool edge that's not level anymore?

## Garage Floors

31. My garage floor has a big lip where it meets the driveway — can you fix that?
32. Can a settling garage floor affect my home's foundation?
33. Can you fix drainage issues where water runs into my garage instead of away from it?

## Roadways & Commercial / Municipal

34. What's the largest infrastructure project you've worked on?
35. Do you work with cities and municipalities directly?
36. How do you handle traffic while working on a road?

## Void Filling & Soil Stabilization

37. How do I know if there's a void under my concrete?
38. What causes voids to form under concrete in Texas?
39. Can you fill voids under a house foundation, or just flatwork?

## Warehouses & Industrial

40. Can you fix warehouse floors without shutting down operations?
41. What kind of weight can the repaired floor handle?
42. Do you work nights and weekends for commercial jobs?

## Texas-Specific / Seasonal

43. Does Texas clay soil make concrete problems worse?
44. Is there a best time of year to have concrete lifted, or does it not matter?
45. After a really dry summer or a wet spring, do you see more calls?
