## Concrete Lifting in Denton — University Town, Real-World Soil Problems

Denton has a mix of everything — historic homes near the square, university-area properties around UNT and TWU, and fast-growing suburbs pushing out in every direction. All of it sits on North Texas clay that expands, contracts, and shifts with the seasons.

We've been lifting concrete across the DFW area for nearly 30 years, and Denton County keeps us plenty busy.

## Common Work in Denton

* **Older homes near downtown and the university district** have driveways and walkways with decades of soil movement underneath. These slabs have been through a lot of Texas weather.
* **Newer subdivisions** along I-35W and 380 where fill dirt under new construction settles within the first few years.
* **Commercial properties** — restaurants, retail, and office buildings in the Rayzor Ranch area, along University Drive, and through the Loop 288 corridor. Parking lots and entrance slabs take heavy daily traffic on reactive soil.
* **Municipal work** — sidewalks, curb and gutter, and public walkways throughout the city.
