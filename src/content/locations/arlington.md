## Concrete Lifting in Arlington — Right in the Heart of DFW

Arlington sits right between Dallas and Fort Worth, and it's got all the concrete problems that come with both. The Tarrant County clay soil expands and contracts with every weather cycle, and decades of development have left a patchwork of old and new concrete sitting on unpredictable ground.

We've been lifting concrete across Arlington for nearly 30 years — from residential neighborhoods to the commercial corridors along I-20 and Division Street.

## What We Fix in Arlington

* **Residential driveways and walkways** in established neighborhoods like Dalworthington Gardens, Pantego, and the subdivisions south of I-20 where decades of soil movement have taken their toll.
* **Sidewalks and trip hazards** in both older neighborhoods and newer master-planned communities. HOA notices about uneven walkways are one of the most common calls we get from Arlington homeowners.
* **Commercial properties** — parking lots, entrance slabs, and sidewalks for retail centers, churches, office buildings, and apartment complexes.
* **Pool decks** — Arlington backyards take a lot of sun and a lot of splash. Both accelerate soil erosion under pool deck concrete.
