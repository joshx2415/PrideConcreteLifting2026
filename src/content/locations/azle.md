## Concrete Lifting in Azle & Springtown

Azle and Springtown sit northwest of Fort Worth where Tarrant and Parker counties meet. The soil out here is a mix of heavy clay and sandy loam — and both types have a habit of shifting, eroding, and washing out from under concrete over time.

* **Residential driveways** — especially where water runoff from sloped lots or poor drainage has washed the soil out from under the slab edges.
