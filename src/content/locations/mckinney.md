## Concrete Lifting in McKinney — Fast Growth, Fast Settling

McKinney has been one of the fastest-growing cities in Texas for years, and that growth means a lot of new concrete sitting on recently graded land. Fill dirt under new construction compacts over time, and when it does, your driveway, sidewalks, and patio slabs go with it.

But it's not just the new stuff. The older parts of McKinney near the historic downtown have their own challenges — decades of clay soil movement under slabs that were poured long before modern soil prep standards.
