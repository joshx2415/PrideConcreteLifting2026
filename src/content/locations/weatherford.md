## Concrete Lifting in Weatherford — Parker County's Soil Has a Mind of Its Own

Weatherford sits right where the Tarrant County clay transitions into the sandy loam and limestone of Parker County. That mix of soil types creates its own set of challenges for concrete — and we've been fixing them for nearly 30 years.

Whether you're in an established neighborhood near the square, out along the 171 corridor, or in one of the newer developments off Santa Fe Drive, the ground under your property has a way of shifting when you're not looking.

## What We See Most in Weatherford

* **Driveways and garage aprons** settling where the soil has washed out from under the edges, especially on properties with slopes or poor drainage.
* **Sidewalks and walkways** with trip hazards caused by soil movement or root intrusion.
* **Pool decks** pulling away from the coping as the soil beneath compacts and erodes.
* **Commercial properties** along Fort Worth Highway and the I-20 corridor with parking lots and entrance slabs that have settled from years of traffic.
