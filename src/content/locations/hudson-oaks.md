## Concrete Lifting in Hudson Oaks & Willow Park

Hudson Oaks and Willow Park sit right along the I-20 corridor west of Fort Worth, where the Tarrant County clay gives way to Parker County's mix of clay, limestone, and sandy loam. That transition zone creates its own set of challenges for concrete — different soil types settling at different rates under the same slab.

We've been serving this area for nearly 30 years and know the ground out here well.

## What We See in Hudson Oaks & Willow Park

* **Residential driveways and garage aprons** — many homes in this area have longer driveways on larger lots, which means more surface area for soil movement to affect.
* **Sidewalks and front entries** settling as the soil shifts, creating trip hazards and code compliance issues.
* **Pool decks** — properties with pools on the slightly sloped terrain common in this area are especially prone to deck settling as water drains and erodes the soil underneath.
* **Commercial concrete** along the I-20 corridor and US-180 — businesses, retail, and restaurants with parking lots and entrance areas that see daily traffic.
