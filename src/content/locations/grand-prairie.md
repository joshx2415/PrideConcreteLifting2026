## Concrete Lifting in Grand Prairie — Where Dallas Meets Fort Worth

Grand Prairie sits right in the middle of the metroplex, straddling Dallas and Tarrant counties. The soil here is textbook North Texas clay — heavy, reactive, and tough on concrete. Years of wet-dry cycles create voids under slabs that lead to settling, cracking, and trip hazards.

We've been lifting concrete across Grand Prairie and the Mid-Cities for nearly 30 years.

* **Residential driveways and walkways** — from older neighborhoods near downtown to newer developments along the south side. Soil movement doesn't discriminate by neighborhood age.
* **Sidewalk trip hazards** — a common issue across Grand Prairie's established subdivisions. HOA notices and city code compliance drive a lot of the calls we get.
* **Commercial and industrial concrete** — Grand Prairie has a strong commercial and industrial corridor. Warehouse floors, parking lots, and loading areas take heavy abuse on reactive soil.
* **Pool decks and patios** — backyard concrete that's settled and now drains the wrong way or creates an uneven surface.
