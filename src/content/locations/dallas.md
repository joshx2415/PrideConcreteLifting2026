## Concrete Lifting in Dallas — From Lake Highlands to Oak Cliff

Dallas is a big city with a lot of concrete, and all of it is sitting on that famous North Texas clay. Whether you're in a 1950s ranch in Lake Highlands, a craftsman in Oak Cliff, or a new build in Far North Dallas, the soil under your property is working against your slab every day.

We've been lifting and stabilizing concrete across Dallas for nearly 30 years. We know the soil, we know the neighborhoods, and we know how to fix the problem without tearing everything up.

## What We See Across Dallas

* **Older homes in East Dallas, Lakewood, and Lake Highlands** often have driveways and walkways that have been settling for decades. The original soil prep under those slabs wasn't always what it should have been, and decades of clay movement has made it worse.
* **North Dallas and Far North Dallas** subdivisions deal with settling on fill dirt that was graded for development. Newer doesn't always mean better when it comes to what's underneath.
* **Commercial properties** across the city — shopping centers, office parks, apartment complexes, and churches — all deal with parking lot settling and sidewalk trip hazards.
* **Municipal infrastructure** — we work with the City of Dallas and property managers on public walkways, curb and gutter repair, and drainage correction.

## The Smarter Fix

We use polyurethane foam injection to lift your concrete back to level and stabilize the soil underneath. It's faster, cleaner, and typically costs 50-70% less than full replacement. Most residential jobs are done in a single day.
