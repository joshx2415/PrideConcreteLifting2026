## Concrete Lifting in Frisco — New Neighborhoods, Same Old Soil

Frisco has transformed from a small town to one of the most desirable suburbs in Texas — but the clay soil underneath hasn't changed. All that new construction sits on graded fill dirt that compacts and shifts over time, and the concrete on top settles with it.

We've been lifting concrete across the DFW metroplex for nearly 30 years, and Frisco's rapid growth has made it one of our busiest service areas.

## What We See in Frisco

* **Driveway and garage apron settling** in neighborhoods across Frisco — from Starwood and Newman Village to Phillips Creek Ranch and Lawler Park. New concrete on new fill is a recipe for early settling.
* **Sidewalk trip hazards** that draw HOA attention. In a community where curb appeal matters, an uneven walkway sticks out.
* **Pool decks** — Frisco homeowners invest heavily in their outdoor spaces. When the deck starts pulling away from the pool, it's a safety issue and an eyesore.
* **Commercial concrete** around the Frisco Square area, the Tollway corridor, and along Lebanon Road where retail and office properties deal with parking lot and entrance slab settling.
