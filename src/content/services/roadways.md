## A Smarter Alternative to Rip and Replace

Closing a lane to tear out and repour concrete means traffic control plans, heavy equipment mobilization, weeks of curing time, and a budget line item that makes everyone wince.

Our polyurethane soil stabilization process offers a practical alternative:

* **Minimal Traffic Disruption:** Our high-density foam cures to 90% strength in 15 minutes. We can treat a section and reopen the lane the same day — often within the hour.
* **Heavy Load Capacity:** We use NCFI Geotechnical industrial-grade polymers. These are the same formulations used on interstate highways and airport runways across Texas. They're built to carry freight traffic and heavy loads.
* **50-70% Cost Savings:** Stabilizing and lifting existing concrete is typically half to two-thirds cheaper than full replacement. That means you can stretch your maintenance budget further and fix more road with the same dollars.

## Deep Injection & Soil Stabilization

A lot of roadway problems aren't just about the slab on top — it's the failed sub-grade underneath. Years of water infiltration, poor compaction, and Texas clay movement hollow out the support structure.

We use deep injection techniques to address this from the ground up. We insert probes to inject expanding polyurethane foam at multiple depths into the soil. This densifies loose, weak material, displaces trapped water, and creates a solid weight-bearing base that prevents future settling.

### Where We Work

We've handled projects across the DFW metroplex and beyond — from county roads in Tarrant and Parker counties to state highway panels, municipal streets in Weatherford, Azle, and Springtown, and airport taxiways. Common applications include:

* **Bridge Approaches & Departures:** Smoothing out the "bump" where the bridge deck meets the road — one of the most common complaints from drivers and a recurring maintenance headache.
* **Highway & Street Panels:** Lifting sunken concrete sections to restore a smooth, safe driving surface.
* **Curb & Gutter Realignment:** Correcting shifted curbs to restore proper stormwater drainage.
* **Airport Runways & Taxiways:** Precision leveling for aviation surfaces where tolerances are tight and downtime is expensive.
