## Fix the "Bump" Before It Breaks Your Tires.

Does entering your garage feel like driving over a curb? That 2-inch "lip" where your driveway meets your garage floor isn't just annoying—it's damaging your vehicle's alignment and tires every single day.

At **Pride Concrete Lifting**, we specialize in leveling garage floors and aprons, ensuring a smooth transition and a dry garage.

## The Problem: Settling Garage Aprons
The "apron" is the section of driveway immediately outside your garage door. In North Texas, this area is notorious for settling due to water runoff eroding the soil against the foundation.

**When the apron sinks, you deal with:**
* **The "Tire Buster":** A sharp, uneven edge that jars your car every time you pull in.
* **Tripping Hazards:** A dangerous step-down that catches toes and causes falls.
* **Water Intrusion:** Instead of draining *away* from your home, the sunken concrete funnels rainwater right under your garage door, rotting the seal and flooding your floor.

## The Solution: Lift It, Don't Replace It
Tearing out a garage floor is a massive headache. You have to move everything out, deal with jackhammers, and wait weeks for new concrete to cure.

**Our Polyurethane Foam Injection is the smarter choice:**
1.  **Minimal Disruption:** In most cases, you don't even need to fully empty your garage. We just need access to the affected area.
2.  **Clean Process:** Our dust-free process ensures your tools, storage boxes, and gym equipment stay clean.
3.  **Immediate Use:** You can park your car back in the garage the same night we finish.

## Correcting Negative Slope (Drainage)
If water pools in the corner of your garage or runs toward the back wall, your slab has settled unevenly.
We can precision-lift specific corners of the slab to restore the proper pitch, ensuring water flows **out** of the garage door, not into your drywall.

## Protect Your Home's Foundation
A settling garage floor can sometimes indicate voids forming near your home's main foundation. By filling these voids with our high-density foam, we stabilize the soil and prevent the problem from spreading to the rest of the house.
