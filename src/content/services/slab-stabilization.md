**We do it differently:**

## Signs You Need Interior Stabilization
It’s not always about new floors. Internal soil erosion can cause your interior slabs to settle independently of your home's main beams.
Look for:
* **Gaps under baseboards:** A space opening up between the floor and your trim.
* **Sticking doors:** Interior doors that suddenly drag on the carpet or won't latch.
* **Cracked drywall:** Stress fractures appearing above door frames.
* **Hollow sounds:** Feeling a "bounce" or hearing a hollow sound when you walk across the floor.

## How It Works
1.  **Scan & Map:** We identify exactly where the voids are beneath your floor.
2.  **Inject:** We inject high-density polyurethane foam which expands to fill the voids and gently lift the floor back to level.
3.  **Patch:** We patch the small injection ports with cement. If you are installing new flooring over the top, the repair is invisible.

## Save Your Renovation
Don't try to fix a sinking slab with "self-leveling" pour-over compound. That adds heavy weight to a slab that is already sinking! Fix the root cause by stabilizing the soil underneath.
