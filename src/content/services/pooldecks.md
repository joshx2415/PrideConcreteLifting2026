## The Dangers of a Sinking Pool Deck
In North Texas, water and soil erosion are constant threats to concrete. Because pool decks are constantly exposed to splashed water and drainage issues, the soil beneath them often washes away or compacts. This leads to:
* **Trip Hazards:** Uneven slabs are dangerous for wet feet and running kids.
* **Coping Separation:** The concrete pulls away from the pool edge, breaking the mastic seal and allowing water to erode the foundation even faster.
* **Drainage Issues:** Water starts pooling *towards* your house or pool rather than draining away.

## The Solution: Clean & Precise Foam Lifting
Repairing a pool deck used to mean tearing out the concrete—a messy, loud process that fills your pool with dust and debris.

We use **Polyurethane Foam Injection**, which is the superior choice for pool areas:
1.  **Pool-Safe & Clean:** We drill tiny holes (dime-sized) and inject foam. There is no heavy machinery and **no concrete dust** to clog your pool filters.
2.  **Moisture Resistant:** Our hydrophobic foam is designed to cure in wet environments. It stabilizes the soil and prevents future washout.
3.  **Immediate Use:** You can walk on the deck and get back in the pool immediately after we finish.

## Infinity Pools: Precision Leveling
For owners of infinity (negative edge) pools, leveling isn't just about looks—it's about function.

If your infinity edge sinks even a fraction of an inch, water will stop flowing over the weir wall evenly. This forces your pumps to work overtime and ruins the visual effect you paid for.
* **Precision Control:** Our foam injection process allows us to lift the structure with millimeter precision, restoring the perfect water sheet flow over the edge.
* **Cost Savings:** restoring the level prevents water loss and reduces electricity bills from strained pumps.
