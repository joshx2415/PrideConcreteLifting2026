## We Lift Your Pool Deck Up - No Floaties Required.

Over time, you may notice your concrete pool decking begin to sink and pull away from your pool - this is an unfortunately common occurence as soils settle underneath your beautiful deck or patio. Pride Concrete Lifting can get you back to safely soaking up the sun.

Nobody wants trip hazards while they're going in for a cannon ball. With Pride Concrete Lifting's polyurethane concrete lifting techniques, this problem can be fixed with minimal invasiveness and time spent. Based on the magnitude and area that has sunken, holes will be drilled so that our expanding foam can be injected underneath allowing the concrete to be lifted up to grade once more.

## Infinity pool not seeming so infinite anymore?

For infinity pools, it's even more critical that they remain level to prevent water from consistently draining and losing your money on water and electricity.
