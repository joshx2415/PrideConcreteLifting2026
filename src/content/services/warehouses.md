## The "Rocking Slab" Problem
Industrial floors take a beating from heavy loads and constant traffic. Over time, the soil beneath the joints can compact or wash away.
When a heavy forklift drives over a joint with no support underneath, the slab tilts or "rocks."

**Why this costs you money:**
* **Equipment Damage:** That "clunk-clunk" sound is the sound of forklift wheels, axles, and transmissions being destroyed.
* **Speed Reduction:** Operators have to slow down to navigate uneven floors, killing your efficiency.
* **Safety Audits:** Tripping hazards and unstable surfaces are major OSHA liabilities.

## The Solution: Joint Stabilization & Undersealing
