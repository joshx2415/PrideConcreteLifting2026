How It Works

We use NCFI-formulated high-density polyurethane foam — the industry gold standard — injected directly beneath the slab through penny-sized holes.

Polyurethane foam cured beneath a concrete slab — lightweight, waterproof, permanent.
1

Inject

Foam is pumped through small, drilled ports beneath the slab.
2

Expand

The foam expands, filling voids and raising the concrete to grade.
3

Stabilize

Cures in 15 minutes. Walk and drive on it immediately.
