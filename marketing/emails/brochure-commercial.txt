HOW IT WORKS
──────────────
We use NCFI-formulated high-density polyurethane foam — the industry gold
standard — injected directly beneath the slab through penny-sized holes.

  1. INJECT — Foam is pumped through small, drilled ports beneath the slab.
  2. EXPAND — The foam expands, filling voids and raising the concrete to grade.
  3. STABILIZE — Cures in 15 minutes. Walk and drive on it immediately.
